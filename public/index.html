How to turn a GIF into a WhatsApp sticker

  1. Choose, drop or paste a GIF, video or image above, or paste a link to a GIF, an X (Twitter) post or a page with a GIF (Pinterest…).
  2. Add a border or caption and trim the clip if you like.
  3. Hold the finished sticker → Copy.
  4. In a WhatsApp chat, hold the message box → Paste and send it as a Custom sticker.
  5. Tap the sticker → Add to Favorites to keep it.

FAQ

Can I make a WhatsApp sticker from a video or an X (Twitter) post?

Yes. Pick a video file, or paste an x.com or twitter.com post link and tap Load. Trim the clip to 10 seconds or less and the app turns it into an animated sticker.

Is it free, and are my files uploaded?

It is free with no sign-up or watermark. Everything is processed in your browser, so your files never leave your device. A pasted link may be fetched by this site's server when the other site blocks browsers; nothing is stored.

Why doesn't WhatsApp offer Custom sticker when I paste?

The file is probably too large. Animated WhatsApp stickers must be under 500 KB; shorten the clip or choose the Light setting and try again.

What about WhatsApp Web or Desktop?

Tap Save WebP to get WhatsApp's native 512×512 animated sticker file, with pack name, author and emoji tags, and drag it into a chat.
